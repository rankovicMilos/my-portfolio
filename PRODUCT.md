# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People evaluating Milos Rankovic's work who may contact him about a project: agencies, designers, and founders looking for a full-stack engineer on a freelance or contract basis. They arrive to see what he has built and decide whether to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio. It shows Milos's projects and background and makes it easy to contact him about a project. Success is a visitor seeing real work first and sending an enquiry.

## Positioning

A full-stack engineer who started on the backend (.NET) and moved across the stack (NestJS, Next.js, React, Angular), with repeated delivery on CMS platforms (Sanity, Umbraco, Optimizely, Strapi).

## Operating Context

Content is edited by Milos in the embedded Sanity Studio at `/studio`. Enquiries arrive through the contact form, which sends email through Resend.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router app in `portfolio/`, content from Sanity, deployed on Vercel.
- Pages: home, work (`/projects`), project detail, about, contact.
- Most projects are under NDA and have no images or live links; the site must present them without looking broken.
- Milos will supply a hero video and more project images after the redesign; the site must look complete without them.
- Undecided: location is not stated anywhere and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name used as the wordmark: Milos Rankovic.
- Visual reference made binding by Milos: the restraint of https://www.1367studio.com/ (not its palette or layout). Fully dark, one accent used rarely.
- Must not read as a clone of 1367 Studio, must not feel empty or cold, must not feel slow or heavy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Eight projects in Sanity; two have images and live URLs (Padel Booking Platform, Website For US Landscaping Business).
- Three experience entries (Vega IT, B-OP Services, self-employed), a bio, skills, education, and a profile photo in Sanity.
- No testimonials, client names, metrics, or press. None may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Work before words: a visitor sees real projects before reading claims.
2. One action: every page leads to starting a project conversation.
3. Only true statements: copy uses facts already in the content.
4. NDA work is first-class: it is presented deliberately, never as a missing picture.
